# RFM-Based Customer Segmentation | Power BI

## Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard project focused on customer segmentation using RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis. The project analyzes customer transaction data to identify valuable customer segments, understand purchasing behaviour, and derive actionable business insights.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Microsoft Excel

## Dataset
- 5,000 transactions
- 500 unique customers
- 9 columns
- Transaction period: January 2024 – December 2025

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | ₹8.53 Crore |
| Total Customers | 500 |
| Total Transactions | 5,000 |
| Champions | 105 |
| Champions' Revenue Contribution | 68.7% |
| Top 20% Customers' Revenue Contribution | 80% |

## Dashboard Pages

**1. Executive Overview**
- Overall revenue and customer performance
- Key business KPIs
- Revenue and transaction trends

**2. Customer Segmentation**
- Customer segment distribution
- Revenue contribution by segment
- Identification of high-value customers

**3. RFM Distribution & Customer Behaviour**
- Recency, Frequency and Monetary analysis
- Customer purchasing patterns
- Customer engagement insights

## Key Insights
- The top 20% of customers contribute approximately 80% of total revenue.
- Champions account for 105 customers and generate 68.7% of total revenue.
- At Risk and Cannot Lose Them segments together represent 81 customers.
- Customer segmentation helps identify opportunities for targeted retention and engagement strategies.

## How to Explore
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file in Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. If prompted, update the source dataset path.
4. Explore the interactive dashboard using filters and slicers.

## Objective
To leverage business intelligence and RFM analysis for understanding customer behaviour, identifying revenue-driving segments, and supporting data-driven customer retention strategies.
